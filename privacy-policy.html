Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

GENZ Feed ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using the GENZ Feed mobile application and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use and share that information, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GENZ Feed, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Information such as your email address and authentication-related information when you create, access, or manage a GENZ Feed account.
  • Profile information: Information associated with your GENZ Feed profile, such as information you choose to provide for your account.
  • App usage information: Information about your interactions with GENZ Feed, including reading activity, followed stories, preferences, and feature usage where applicable.
  • Saved content and preferences: Information needed to provide features that allow you to save, follow, organize, or personalize content within GENZ Feed.
  • Device and technical information: Basic technical information that may be available to the application or its service providers, such as device type, operating system, application version, and diagnostic information used to maintain application reliability and security.

GENZ Feed does not require you to provide information that is not necessary for the features you choose to use.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide and operate GENZ Feed.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account sessions.
  • Provide profile and account-management functionality.
  • Personalize the reading experience.
  • Remember user-selected preferences and content-related choices.
  • Provide followed-story and saved-content functionality.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate misuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve application reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and account-related inquiries.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or allow access to limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate GENZ Feed. These providers may support functions such as authentication, database and data storage, application infrastructure, hosting, security, analytics or other services required to operate the application.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or integrity of our users, services, or property.

4. Third-Party Services

GENZ Feed relies on third-party service providers and infrastructure to provide certain parts of the service. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

These services may include authentication, database storage, infrastructure, application services, hosting, diagnostics, and related technical functionality.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable agreements. Where appropriate, we encourage users to review the privacy practices of relevant third-party services.

5. Advertising and Featured Content

GENZ Feed may display advertisements or featured content within the application.

Information associated with advertising functionality may be processed as necessary to display, deliver, measure, or maintain advertisements and featured content. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Any advertising-related data handling is subject to the applicable privacy practices of the services used to provide advertising functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide GENZ Feed, maintain legitimate operational and security purposes, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or misuse, and enforce applicable agreements.

When an account is deleted, we delete the account and associated application data that is required to be deleted, subject to any information that we are legally permitted or required to retain.

Where information must be retained for legitimate reasons such as legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution, that information may be retained for the period required for that purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

GENZ Feed provides account deletion both inside the application and through an external web resource.

If you have a GENZ Feed account, you can permanently request deletion of your account and associated account data directly from within the application.

In the GENZ Feed app, go to:

Profile → Account → Delete Account

If you cannot access the application, you can use our external account deletion page to request deletion:

GENZ Feed Account Deletion

The external account deletion page provides an additional method for users who cannot access the application and explains how to contact us to request account deletion.

When an account deletion request is completed, the GENZ Feed account and associated user data that is required to be deleted will be removed from our systems, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory retention requirements.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleting the app from your device or logging out does not by itself delete your GENZ Feed account.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access to account and service data is controlled using appropriate authentication and authorization mechanisms.

However, no method of electronic transmission, internet communication, or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

GENZ Feed is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in violation of applicable requirements, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is handled.

You may exercise applicable rights by using the account-management features available in GENZ Feed or by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

For account deletion, you may use the in-app Delete Account option or our external account deletion resource:

https://supportgenzfeed.github.io/genz-feed-account/

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to GENZ Feed, our data practices, legal requirements, or our services.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page. We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
