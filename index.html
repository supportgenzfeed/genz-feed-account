Delete your GENZ Feed account

You can permanently delete your GENZ Feed account and associated account data directly from the GENZ Feed Android app.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

1
Open the GENZ Feed app and sign in to your account.
2
Open your Profile and select Account.
3
Select Delete Account and review the confirmation message.
4
Confirm the deletion request to permanently delete your account.

Request deletion if you cannot access the app

If you cannot sign in or access the in-app account deletion option, you can contact our support team to request account deletion.

External account deletion request

Send an email from the email address associated with your GENZ Feed account. Include your request to permanently delete your account.

Request Account Deletion

For security, requests may require verification of account ownership before deletion is completed.

What will be deleted?

  • Your GENZ Feed user account.
  • Your profile information associated with the account.
  • Your account-associated reading or activity data, where applicable.
  • Other user-owned data linked to the account, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
